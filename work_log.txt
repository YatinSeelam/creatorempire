the browser tab said "UGC brand deals in 30 days, or your $500 back". that was
app/layout.tsx reading the ugc flows offer out of lib/content.ts, and it turned
out to be one of about forty places.

identity and seo
- app/layout.tsx: title is the product's name, nothing else. no keyword list, no
  price, no guarantee, no og image. robots noindex/nofollow: `/` redirects to
  the dashboard and everything behind it wants a seat, so there is nothing here
  for a crawler and an invite-only roster in search results is a leak.
- app/robots.ts: disallow everything. it was open with private prefixes carved
  out, which is right for a marketing site and wrong for this.
- deleted app/sitemap.ts (listed /, /sign-up, /editors and portfolios, most of
  them 404s here), public/llms.txt (a ugcflows.com pitch written for ai
  crawlers), public/og.png and scripts/og-image.mjs (both drew the offer).

2026-08-26 — dashboard: what the month lands on, and eight accuracy fixes behind it

the ask: show a projection of end-of-month earnings including bonuses, organised well.

new panel on /dashboard, above the four tiles and deliberately outside the range
picker: "on track for $X", booked vs still to come, a bar whose fill is the
booked share with a tick marking where the month actually is, base and bonus as
separate halves, six months of history with this month's forecast drawn as a
dashed cap, a month-over-month line, then a row per deal. `earnings.outlook` and
`earnings.months` were both already being computed by loadEarnings and thrown
away; the panel is what finally reads them. page flipped off flame/rounded-xl
onto ink/squared to match the rest of the product.

- lib/deals.ts: new monthlyBonusOutlook(), the counterpart to monthlyBaseOutlook.
  bonus cannot be read off a rate sheet, so it is a run rate over the trailing 7
  days carried across the days the month has left, floored at what is booked.
  off entirely when a milestone rule is anywhere on the deal: the rate is
  measured on a deal-level total that cannot tell a $2,000 tier landing on
  tuesday apart from a fortnight of cpm, and multiplying that day forward
  forecasts the same jackpot two or three more times.
- lib/deals.ts: monthlyBaseOutlook now refuses to forecast a deal carrying a
  `tier_mode = replace` rule. deal_earnings drops replaced videos out of
  base_videos, so booked base reads 0 forever, which the quota maths read as
  "nothing posted yet" and turned into a full month of pay never owed. also
  takes only the days of the month the deal is actually live, because nothing
  flips `status` when `ends_on` passes and a deal that finished on the 8th was
  forecasting a full month of work nobody was going to do.
- lib/dash-server.ts: one bonus_rules read builds a per-deal digest (continuous,
  milestone, replacesBase, closesOn). the run rate is capped at the deal's
  ends_on and at the day its last absolute-window rule closes, because carrying
  a rate past a hard stop bills for days the rule cannot pay on.
- lib/dash-server.ts: an rpc resolves with {data: null, error} rather than
  throwing, and `?? []` turned that into "everyone earned nothing that day". a
  failed BASELINE therefore printed a deal's whole lifetime under a "last 30
  days" label. failed days are remembered now, between() returns 0 rather than
  inflating, the forecast steps aside, and the page says the numbers are short.
- lib/dash-server.ts: the views window now carries a baseline forward across the
  sync interval instead of using the earliest reading inside the window.
  snapshots land every 3 days, so `today` was comparing today's reading against
  itself and reporting 0 views roughly two days in three.
- lib/dash-server.ts: per-deal views/posts now respect `videos.counts`, so
  un-ticking a video stops it counting here the way it already does on /deals
  and in every payout. two "views" columns one click apart no longer disagree.
- lib/dash-server.ts: posts queued/failed get their own unwindowed counts. they
  are stated as "right now" and were being bounded by the range picker, which
  hid a post scheduled for next week behind a 30-day floor on created_at.
- lib/dash-server.ts: connected accounts counts only the four platforms this
  product tracks. upload-post returns every network a person ever logged into,
  so a creator with linkedin and x attached read "4 of 2 connected".
- lib/dash-server.ts: postedInRange reads the exact count header rather than
  rows.length, which a page cap truncates first.
- lib/dash-server.ts: deleted DashboardTrend, postedThisWeek and the growth
  maths behind them. four queries including an rpc, on every dashboard load,
  feeding fields no page has ever rendered.
- app/(dash)/dashboard/page.tsx: the "earned" tile stops splitting into paid vs
  accruing. paidCents is every payout ever logged and the total is one window,
  so past a creator's first month it read "all of it is paid" on a day money was
  actively being earned. it shows the flat/bonus split of the window instead.

found by two audit subagents against the live schema; both reports verified
before acting.
